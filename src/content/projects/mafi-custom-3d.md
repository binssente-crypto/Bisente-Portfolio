### Overview
For my university capstone, I partnered with Michael Anthony's Fashion Inc. to solve one of their biggest operational bottlenecks: manual sketches and slow in-person customization reviews. Over the course of nearly a year, I built an interactive 3D and canvas tailoring platform where customers can configure fabrics, view custom garment angles, tweak collar and sleeve configurations, and submit order specifications directly to the cutting floor.

### Key Features
- **Interactive 3D Apparel Customizer**: Real-time garment visualization with color, texture, and trim selection.
- **Automated Order Pipeline**: Customer selections translate directly into production work orders.
- **Admin Dashboard**: Comprehensive management of fabric inventories, pricing formulas, and custom job statuses.
- **Customer Portal**: Order tracking with automated email updates powered by PHPMailer.
