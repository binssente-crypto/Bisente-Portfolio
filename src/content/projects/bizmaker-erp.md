### Overview
What started as a focused payroll calculator in April 2026 quickly expanded into a full-scale HRIS and ERP SaaS, designed and built entirely by me. Built with Vue 3, TypeScript, and a robust Django REST backend, BizMaker automates 2316 tax schedules, SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG statutory deductions, biometric clock-in backfills, and multi-tier tenant management for Philippine businesses.

### Key Features
- **Statutory Tax & Deductions**: Precise Philippine BIR 2316 tax schedules, SSS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG deduction rules.
- **Biometric Integration**: Automated attendance sync with backfill algorithms for missed punches and shift variations.
- **Multi-Tenant Architecture**: Secure tenant isolation with customizable organizational payroll rules.
- **Reporting Engine**: Instant export of employee payslips, government remittance summaries, and bank payroll sheets via SheetJS and jsPDF.
