### Overview
During my 4-month software engineering internship with Gamma Oracle Dimension Inc. (Feb 1 – Jun 1, 2026), I collaborated closely with a fellow developer intern to build the official educational portal for Tax Leaders Circle PH (tlcph.online). Together we engineered the dynamic certificate studio, publication reader, and administrative management tools.

### Key Contributions
- **Certificate Studio**: Dynamic accreditation certificate generation with verifiable signatures via FPDI/FPDF.
- **Interactive Tax Library**: Digital publication reader using Turn.js and PDF.js with responsive page-flip reading.
- **Admin Dashboard**: Seminar reservation management, attendee verification, and lesson categories.
- **Transactional Notifications**: Automated email delivery of seminar confirmations and certificates using PHPMailer.
