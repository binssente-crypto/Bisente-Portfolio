### Overview
Point of Sale and inventory system custom-built for Bubble Hideout, a dining spot located in CAA, Las Piñas.

### Key Features
- **Cashier & Order Management**: Rapid order placement with live pricing, customizations, and split checks.
- **Sales Analytics**: Real-time daily, weekly, and monthly sales reporting with profit breakdown.
- **Account Control & Audit**: Role-based access control with login history and archival functionality.
- **Inventory Tracking**: Stock level monitoring with automatic deduction upon order completion.
