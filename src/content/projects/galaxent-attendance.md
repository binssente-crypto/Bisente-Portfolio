### Overview
Galaxent Perfume needed an orderly way to track staff daily attendance and shift schedules across their retail branches. I built a custom PHP and MySQL attendance dashboard that lets employees log their hours and gives administrators a calendar view of who showed up, who was late, and monthly attendance records.

### Key Features
- **Daily Clock-In & Verification**: Clean employee time-logging interface with timestamp verification.
- **Visual Calendar Grid**: High-level monthly overview highlighting absences, on-time arrivals, and overtime.
- **Admin Management**: User profile controls, password updates, and CSV record exports.
