### Overview
Upon transitioning to my full-time role at AAA and Co., CPAs starting June 1, 2026, I took on lead development for Gamma Oracle Dimension's enterprise project management and cost control system. The application handles multi-project budgeting, contractor payment schedules, expense verification, and automated audit trails backed by PostgreSQL and Supabase.

### Key Features
- **Project Cost Governance**: Budget tracking, change-order tracking, and automated cost variance calculations.
- **Enterprise Security & Compliance**: Strict role-based permissions, JWT authentication, rate limiting, and Helmet headers.
- **Financial Ledger Sync**: PostgreSQL sync service for transaction reconciliation and project milestones.
- **Document Management**: Centralized hub for contractor contracts, tax withholdings, and verifiable payment orders.
