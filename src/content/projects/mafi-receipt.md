### Overview
A local garment and sewing manufacturer was spending hours handwriting delivery receipts and client invoices. I put together a lightweight Node and Express tool backed by SQLite so their front office could draft, calculate, and print clean receipts in seconds without dealing with complex cloud subscriptions.

### Key Features
- **Instant DR & Receipt Generation**: Pre-formatted layout matching BIR invoice and delivery standards.
- **Local SQLite Persistence**: Zero-setup offline-first storage so records remain accessible even during network outages.
- **Print Optimization**: Pixel-precise print stylesheets for thermal and dot-matrix receipt printers.
