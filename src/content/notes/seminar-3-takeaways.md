The Packetworx and KadaKareer workshop was a refreshing deep dive into low-power wide-area networks (LoRaWAN). As someone primarily focused on web and backend applications, understanding how physical sensor nodes transmit tiny telemetry payloads across kilometers was fascinating.

Key takeaways:
- **Designing for constraints**: Unlike web sockets where bandwidth is cheap, LoRaWAN forces you to optimize every byte in the radio payload.
- **Gateway architectures**: Seeing how distributed gateways ingest packet bursts and forward them to central network servers gave me great ideas for future telemetry dashboards.
- **Bridging hardware with cloud APIs**: Connecting sensor telemetry to RESTful backends opens up countless real-world applications for logistics, agriculture, and facility monitoring.
