DICT Regional Office 8's Cyber-Shield webinar focused on practical threat landscapes rather than abstract theory. Spending four hours analyzing attack vectors gave me a stronger appreciation for how quickly minor oversights turn into security emergencies.

Key takeaways:
- **Defense in depth over perimeter walls**: Never assume internal network services or behind-the-firewall calls are trustworthy. Validate every request payload.
- **Session and token hygiene**: Keep JWT expiration windows short and enforce rotation strategies to blunt credential harvesting attempts.
- **Incident awareness**: Knowing the exact steps to isolate an compromised service is just as valuable as writing unit tests. Incident response isn't only for enterprise SOC teams—it applies to freelance deployments too.
